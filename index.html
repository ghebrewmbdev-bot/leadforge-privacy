LeadForge Privacy Policy

App: LeadForge: Leadership Skills (package com.weegix.leadforge)
Publisher: Weegix Technology Solutions LLC, Illinois, USA
Effective date: November 6, 2026

LeadForge is a leadership learning app. We built it to keep your information on your device. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with data.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect your personal information. LeadForge has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers that receive your data. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

2. Information stored on your device

To work properly, the app stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your learning progress (completed lessons, journal days, and scenarios) and your streak.
  • Your settings, such as your daily reminder on or off and the reminder time.
  • Your quiz results and achievement badges.

This data is never sent to us or to any third party. It is removed if you use the "Reset progress" option in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

3. Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. They are created and delivered by your device. No notification data is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your device settings at any time.

4. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

LeadForge does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and it does not use your data to build a profile about you. We do not sell or share personal data.

5. Purchases

LeadForge is currently free to use. If paid features are introduced in a future version, purchases will be processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Google's handling of payment information is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

6. Links to other websites

The app may open links, such as the Weegix website or your email app, in your browser or another app. Those sites and apps have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

7. Children's privacy

LeadForge is intended for adults and general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Data security

Your data stays in the app's private storage on your device and is protected by your device's security. Because we do not hold your data, we cannot access, recover, or delete it for you. Keep your device updated and protected with a screen lock.

9. Your rights

Because all data is on your device and under your control, you can view, change, or delete it at any time using the in-app reset option or by uninstalling the app. If you have questions about your privacy, contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.
